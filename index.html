What's inside the book?

Thirty pages of child-led station cards, each tied to a real seasonal dish. Every card lists the exact job your child does, the safe tool to use, and how to set up the moment so it stays calm and mess-controlled while you finish the meal.

How do I use it during a busy meal?

Pick the dish you're already making, flip to its matching station card, and hand your child their one job. The steps take seconds to set up, so you keep cooking while they stay happily engaged beside you — not underfoot.

Is it really safe for a 2-year-old?

Yes. Every station is sorted by age-appropriateness with built-in safety guidance, so you never guess. Younger toddlers pour and stir, while older preschoolers cut soft foods with kid-safe tools — always tasks that match what their little hands can genuinely handle.
